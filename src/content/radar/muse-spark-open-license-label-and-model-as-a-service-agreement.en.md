## What changed

Meta published the weights of Muse Spark Open under the Muse Model License, with a release date of October 15, 2026 [s1]. The model is natively multimodal, with 816B total parameters (42B active) and a 1M-token context window [s2]. On Hugging Face, you log in to review the conditions before you can access the weights [s3].

## The label is the clause that reaches you

I think the label, more than the revenue threshold, is the clause product teams should plan around. Two conditions come with the license. Each product or service that uses the model or any Derivatives must prominently display "Built with Muse Spark Open", in its user interface or in its documentation, website or settings menu [s1][s5]. A licensee that gives third parties access to AI models via API, and whose revenue with its affiliates exceeds 20 million US dollars over twelve months, must notify Meta and sign a separate written agreement before offering the model or its Derivatives in that business [s1]. MarkTechPost reads internal use, research and powering your own product features as exempt from the revenue clause [s4]. In my reading, the label has no revenue floor, and it follows Derivatives.

> [!CONFIRMED]
> The license defines Derivatives as any AI model "created, trained, modified (including fine-tuned), distilled, or otherwise improved using the Software or any outputs or results thereof" [s1].

> [!INFERRED]
> In my reading, a smaller model you train on synthetic data generated by Muse Spark Open falls under that definition, so the product that ships it inherits the label. A lawyer may read the two clauses differently.

## The strongest objection

The label is cheap: one line in a settings menu or a docs page [s1]. I agree about the line. What it demands is knowing which models carry it. Once Muse outputs are mixed into a training set without a record, nobody can say which downstream models owe the label.

## Impact on your team

My call: if you plan to use Muse Spark Open as a teacher or as a synthetic data generator, record which datasets carry its outputs before they are mixed into a training set, and plan the label into the docs of each model trained on them. If you serve models via API, the threshold counts the revenue of the licensee and its affiliates [s1]; in my view, a large company that starts a small model API crosses it the day it opens. Get the license signed off by your counsel before anyone clicks through the Hugging Face gate [s3].
