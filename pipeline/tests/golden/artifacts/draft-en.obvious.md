Artificial intelligence is changing software engineering. In this post we look at what AI
is and at how developers use it today. It is a big topic and it matters to everyone who
writes code.

## What AI is

AI is a branch of computer science that builds systems able to do tasks that once needed a
human. Modern AI is built on large language models that are trained on very large amounts of
text. Because these models can read and write text, they can also read and write code, which
is useful for programmers. There are many kinds of AI and the field keeps growing.

## How developers use AI

Developers use AI to write code faster. They also use it to find bugs and to explain code
they do not know. Many editors now include an AI assistant, and many teams have started to
try these tools. As the technology gets better, more developers will use it. In short, AI is
a helpful tool that is becoming a normal part of software engineering, and it is here to stay.
